# OJUEWA — The Art of Becoming

Concept: contemporary editorial beauty atelier with a deep plum (#351E30), champagne (#F4E9DE), muted gold (#B99872), dusty rose (#C78F9D), and ivory (#FFF9F3) visual language.

Cormorant Garamond headlines and DM Sans body typography give the site a fashion-editorial direction. The layered campaign hero connects to a tactile service selector, demonstration mirror, editorial gallery, beauty craftsmanship story, and guided appointment request.

## Assets needed before launch
- Real studio and founder/team photos
- Consent-cleared portfolio and actual before/after pairs
- Verified services, contact details, and opening hours
- Persistent hosted database and customer notification channel

Current photos are explicitly illustrative; the mirror is a color-grading comparison, never represented as a client transformation.
